Dot Product: I used the dot product to calculate the distance of the player from the ghosts and gargoyles.
Linear Interpolation: I used linear interpolation to change the particle colors back and forth from red to blue like police sirens.
Particles and Soudns: Using the dot product, when the player gets to close to an enemy who is facing their direction, an alarm sound plays and warning particles appear.
I worked alone for this project.
